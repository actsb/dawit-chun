Journey & Honors

From a president's award
to a processor — in five years.

KAIST President's Award at 13. University at 15. Top 50 nationwide at 17. A first-author processor submission at 18 — with the medals, stages, and cities along the way.

2021

KAIST President's Award, age 13

Early recognition in Korea's gifted-education track — the start of taking hardware seriously.

2022

National gold · first hackathon win · teaching abroad

Gold Prize, KSME National Student Design & Engineering Competition. 1st Place, Geumcheon Hackathon (age 14). First summer teaching Arduino and C to 30 Cambodian science teachers (KOICA).

2023

University at 15

Admitted to Taejae University's inaugural cohort — youngest student, top entrance score. Gold Prize at the Aramco World Coding Cup. Selected into KAIST's DICIA masterclass (A+). Visiting student in bioinformatics at UNIST.

2024

Research gears engage

Silver Medal, International Robot Olympiad Autonomous Driving. Excellence Paper Award, KSCI Winter Conference. Co-authored ChatGPT: Practical AI for the Classroom. Began the Ubion research internship (Dec).

2025

The big year

🏆 1st Place worldwide, ACM CHI Student Design Competition. Talent Award of Korea — top 50 nationwide, entire 200M KRW prize donated. Gold Medal, IRO Autonomous Driving. Oxford-Stanford-Taejae Fellowship — 1st in the AI Policy Debate, presented to Korea's Deputy Prime Minister. SNU DYROS bootcamp. Vice Student Body President. DeepCo Board demonstrated at COEX.

2026

The processor ships

PDPM submitted to IEEE ICCD 2026 (first author). iPhoneme brain-to-text preprint on arXiv. Patent application filed. TA for two courses. Preparing PhD applications for Fall 2027.

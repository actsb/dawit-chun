About

From digital logic
to learning systems.

I entered university at fifteen. Three years later, I am the first author of an IEEE ICCD 2026 submission — a RISC-V processor that morphs its pipeline depth at runtime under an online-learned controller, written in ~4,000 lines of Verilog and validated on two FPGAs.

The result I am proudest of is not the accuracy number, but the reformulation behind it: recognizing that twelve legal configurations collapse to three non-dominated choices turned a 60-weight classifier into a 10-weight regressor that is simultaneously more accurate and 6× cheaper in hardware. That is how I like to work — find the structure of the problem, then build the machine that exploits it.

I work fluently across the stack: digital design (Verilog, SystemVerilog, VHDL), FPGA flows (Yosys/nextpnr, Vivado), ML systems (TensorFlow, OpenCV), robotics (ROS), and embedded hardware (PCB design, ESP32, Raspberry Pi). I am preparing to apply for PhD programs in ECE and CS (Fall 2027), aiming to make learning a first-class citizen inside computer architecture.

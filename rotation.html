Global Rotation

One degree,
lived in six cities.

I study at Taejae University — Korea's answer to Minerva. From sophomore year, my entire cohort rotates through five countries: Seoul → Tokyo → San Francisco → New York → Shenzhen → Beijing. For someone who builds robots, the route reads like a map of the industry itself.

What Taejae is — in one paragraph, for readers who haven't met it

  • A four-year university founded in 2023 (accredited by Korea's Ministry of Education), built around a Minerva-style global rotation — I am part of the founding cohort of 32 students, admitted at 15 as the youngest with the top entrance score
  • 100% English instruction · every class a live seminar capped at 20 students on an active-learning platform · fully residential in every city
  • In each rotation city we live at a partner university and run a semester-long Civic Project — a team study of a real local urban problem, presented to faculty and city officials

Sem 1–3 · 2023–24 Seoul — foundations and first machines

Taejae University home campus, Jongno-gu

Three foundation semesters: physics, math, and the first university research — the JKSCI smart-window papers and the start of the Ubion autonomous-vehicle internship (Dec 2024) all happened from Seoul.

Sem 4 · Spring 2025 Tokyo — the semester of the world title

Hosted at Kanda University of International Studies, greater Tokyo (Chiba)

Our first international rotation — living in the homeland of industrial robotics. While based in Japan, our team went to Yokohama for ACM CHI 2025 and won 1st place worldwide in the Student Design Competition. That summer also brought the Oxford-Stanford-Taejae Fellowship and the SNU DYROS robotics bootcamp.

Sem 5 · Fall 2025 San Francisco — a semester in the Valley

Hosted at the University of San Francisco — resident on campus, taking USF–Taejae co-developed courses

Living an hour from Stanford, Berkeley, and the VLA startups rewriting robotics (Physical Intelligence, Skild). This was the semester my two threads fused: the PDPM morphable-processor work took shape here, while the Valley's robot-learning wave set the direction I'd chase next.

Sem 6 · Spring 2026 New York — the publishing semester

Hosted at Pace University, Lower Manhattan

The semester the papers shipped: the iPhoneme brain-to-text preprint went to arXiv (April 2026), the PDPM ICCD paper was finalized (later accepted as a Regular Paper), and the patent application was filed — from the city of NYU's GRAIL and Columbia's robotics labs.

Summer 2026 Seoul interlude — ROBOTIS

Between rotations: the Humanoid Software Team at ROBOTIS — benchmarking 2026's SOTA VLA and world models on real manipulators, building Prior-ACT and IGEN, and contributing upstream. The internship that made physical AI my main road.

Sem 7 · Fall 2026 — upcoming Shenzhen — application season in the robot capital

Partner: Shenzhen University

Next: the city of DJI and UBTECH — the place where robot hardware actually gets made at scale. For someone who has lived inside a robot company's software team, a semester in the world's hardware capital is the perfect field study — while I submit PhD applications from across the Pacific. Photos and Civic Project notes will appear here as the semester unfolds.

Sem 8 · Spring 2027 — upcoming Beijing — the closing semester

Partner: Peking University

The final rotation — in the city of Unitree's humanoid rivals and China's embodied-AI push — while managing PhD interviews across a 15-hour time difference to the U.S. West Coast. Six cities, five countries, one degree: by graduation, "adapting to a new environment" won't be a hope in my SOP; it will be my transcript.
